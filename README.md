I am an **AI Researcher** with over three years of industry experience in **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**, and **Educational AI**. My work focuses on developing reliable AI systems with robust reasoning capabilities for real-world applications.

My research spans **mathematical reasoning**, **LLM evaluation**, **error detection**, **uncertainty estimation**, and **preference-based learning**. I have also worked on AI tutoring systems, multilingual evaluation, human response modeling, code generation, and multimodal AI applications in education.

Currently, I am interested in **Reinforcement Learning for LLMs**, **reliable generative AI**, **AI alignment**, and **uncertainty-aware reasoning**. My long-term goal is to develop AI systems that not only generate accurate outputs, but also recognize their own uncertainty and make reliable decisions in complex real-world settings.

I am open to research collaborations across diverse areas of AI. Please feel free to reach out if you are interested in working together.
